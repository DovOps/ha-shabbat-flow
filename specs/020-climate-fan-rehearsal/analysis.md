# Analysis
A duration-based nap can exceed a short observance day; clip newly proposed durations visibly, while rejecting inverted explicit clock choices locally. Climate state Off does not reliably identify the previous operating mode. Capture a known active mode explicitly and bind it to registry identity; never use a service fallback that guesses a mode. Service acknowledgment does not establish preservation: confirm power, context and settings after the command. Native fan power may reset speed, so review and pass the captured speed/preset where supported. Scheduled execution remains independently restricted to lighting.
